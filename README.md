# Awesome-Credential-Management

# Top Credential Management Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Digital Badges, Open Badges, Micro-Credentials, Certificate Issuance, Verification & Learner Portfolios*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Credential Management**. These systems issue, manage, verify, and display digital badges and certificates—often based on Open Badges or Verifiable Credentials—for education, workforce, and professional recognition.

**Examples** include Credly, Accredible, Certifier, Badgr, CertifyMe, Virtualbadge.io, Open Badge Factory, Certopus, Canvas Credentials, and PeopleCert (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Commercial credential platforms dominate high-volume and enterprise issuance. Strong open foundations exist via the **Open Badges** standard, **Certo**, **Badge Engine**, and related open issuer stacks. This section expands those while remaining realistic about the commercial gap for large networks.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Credly](https://www.credly.com/)**  
  Leading digital credential and badge network (Pearson) widely used for IT and professional certification ecosystems with strong employer recognition.

- **[Accredible](https://www.accredible.com/)**  
  Enterprise digital credential platform for high-volume issuance, design, integrations, and distribution of badges and certificates.

- **[Certifier](https://certifier.io/)**  
  Modern certificate and badge issuance platform focused on speed, design, and high-volume one-off and program credentials.

- **[Badgr / Canvas Credentials](https://www.instructure.com/)**  
  Digital badging and micro-credentialing platform (now part of the Instructure / Canvas / Parchment ecosystem) built on Open Badges.

- **[CertifyMe](https://certifyme.online/)**  
  Digital credential platform emphasizing open standards, structured learning journeys, and verifiable credentials.

- **[Virtualbadge.io](https://www.virtualbadge.io/)**  
  Digital badge and certificate issuance platform for training providers, events, and organizations.

- **[Open Badge Factory](https://openbadgefactory.com/)**  
  European-oriented Open Badges platform for creating, issuing, and managing digital badges aligned with open standards.

- **[Certopus](https://certopus.com/)**  
  Certificate and credential management platform for automated issuance and verification workflows.

- **[Canvas Credentials](https://www.instructure.com/)**  
  Canvas-native digital credentialing (Badgr lineage) for institutions already using the Canvas LMS.

- **[PeopleCert](https://www.peoplecert.org/)**  
  Professional certification body and credential ecosystem (ITIL, PRINCE2, and related) with digital credential delivery.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[Certo](https://github.com/schroedinger-hat/certo)**  
  Open-source platform for issuing, managing, and verifying credentials using Open Badges 3.0 and W3C Verifiable Credentials.

- **[Badge Engine (Digital Promise)](https://github.com/digital-promise/badge-engine)**  
  Open-source digital badging technology for creating and managing badge programs aligned with Open Badges 3.0.

- **[Badgr Server (legacy open core)](https://github.com/concentricsky/badgr-server)**  
  Historic open-source Open Badges server (AGPL) that underpinned Badgr; useful reference for standards-compliant issuance.

- **[Open Badges specification & tooling](https://www.imsglobal.org/activity/digital-badges)**  
  1EdTech / IMS Open Badges standard and related open validators, bakers, and libraries for interoperable credentials.

- **[W3C Verifiable Credentials open stacks](https://github.com/)**  
  Open libraries and wallets for Verifiable Credentials that intersect with modern Open Badges 3.0 implementations.

- **[Open badge image bakers and validators](https://github.com/)**  
  Community tools for embedding badge metadata in images and verifying assertion integrity.

- **[Self-hosted issuer prototypes](https://github.com/)**  
  Lightweight open projects for issuing Open Badges from events, workshops, and small training programs.

- **[Learner backpack / portfolio open demos](https://github.com/)**  
  Experimental open backpacks for collecting and displaying badges from multiple issuers.

- **[Documentation and Open Badges playbooks](https://openbadges.org/)**  
  Guides for designing badge systems, aligning with standards, and deploying open issuers.

- **[Community credential frameworks](https://github.com/)**  
  Shared taxonomies and badge class templates for skills and achievements under open licenses.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Issuing with **Certo** or **Badge Engine** for standards-compliant, self-hosted programs.
- Validating and baking badges with open Open Badges 3.0 tools.
- Combining open issuers with W3C Verifiable Credentials for portable, learner-controlled credentials.
- Accepting that large public badge networks, enterprise integrations, high-volume design studios, and employer recognition still favor commercial platforms (Credly, Accredible, Certifier, Canvas Credentials, Open Badge Factory, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on portability, standards compliance, and freedom from proprietary lock-in.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Define badge classes under Open Badges 3.0 → issue with Certo or Badge Engine → host assertions openly → let earners collect in open backpacks. Suitable for communities, nonprofits, and education innovators. Large certification bodies and enterprises typically use commercial credential platforms.
